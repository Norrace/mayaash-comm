## Employee Frontend Usage

### How Workers Get Their ID Cards

1. **Visit the website**
2. **Select Role**: Team Leader or Brand Ambassador
3. **Enter ID Number**: First 5 digits (e.g., 29843)
4. **Select Worker**: Choose from dropdown (shows matching workers)
5. **Select Territory**: From dropdown (all 26 available)
6. **Select Region**: MERU MOUNTAIN (only option)
7. **Upload Photo**: Passport size photo
8. **View QR Code**: Auto-generated for verification
9. **Pay**: Use M-Pesa or download code (50 KSH)
10. **Download**: Get ID card after payment
